My First Case Competition

IIM Bangalore Case Competition · Track 2: Policy & Regulation · Team entry

I took part in the IIM Bangalore Case Competition as part of a team. Our topic was DigiYatra 2.0: how DigiYatra's identity verification could extend from airports to healthcare and hospitality. Our idea in one line was “Expand DigiYatra's identity layer, not its data footprint.”

This was my first case competition. I learned how to approach a business problem, research an opportunity, structure recommendations and present a solution.

Result: we didn't qualify
