Sapne

written by Som Sharma

When I was 9 years old, my goal was to become a great scientist.

Maybe I had watched a movie about a scientist, or maybe I had heard that scientists were cool. I don't really remember. But that's what I wanted to become.

As I grew older, my goal changed.

I wanted to become an actor.

I thought actors got the most attention. So I started acting in theatre shows, and I even got the opportunity to act in a movie. I won a few awards too.

Then COVID happened.

Everything stopped.

And when I got a mobile phone during COVID, I found another dream.

I wanted to become a gamer who streamed on YouTube.

So I uploaded gaming videos.

And then, after some time, I got bored.

I left gaming.

That has been my journey of changing goals.

And I know what you're thinking:

“What is your goal now?”

I won't tell you.

Because honestly...

I don't have one.

I don't know what I want to become in the future.

Some days I think about going back to acting, making movies and uploading them on YouTube.

Some days I think about becoming a writer and writing books.

Sometimes I think about becoming a businessman.

And sometimes I imagine something completely different.

Having a wife and children. Coming home after working for hours. Having dinner with my family. And just feeling happy.

That's why I don't know what I want to become.

And I don't think anyone really knows.

Someone can study engineering and become a stand-up comedian loved by everyone. (Samay Raina)

Someone can be born in a small town in Tamil Nadu and become the Missile Man of India. (Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam)

We don't always know where our lives are going to take us.

Sometimes you want something so badly that you think getting it would be the best thing in the world.

But when you don't get it, you think you've failed.

Maybe you haven't.

Maybe life is just taking you somewhere you haven't imagined yet.

As Zakir Khan once said:

“Agar tumhe aaj ye shirt chahiye to duniya itni haramzaadi hai ki aaj to nahi hi milegi, lekin agle din pata chalega wo shirt itni zaruri thi bhi nahi, kyunki ek behtar suit tumhara intezaar kar raha tha.”
